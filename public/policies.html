Returns & replacement policy

  • If your belt arrives damaged, defective, the wrong item, or doesn’t fit, email us within days of delivery. We’ll replace it free, or refund you if a replacement isn’t possible.

Refund & cancellation policy

  • You can cancel your order any time before it is dispatched – email us with your order number.

  • If money was deducted but your order was not confirmed, it is refunded automatically by Razorpay within 5–7 working days.
